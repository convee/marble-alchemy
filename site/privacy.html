Privacy note

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Marble Alchemy is playable without an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details, and we do not read file contents.

Analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics reports page views, device type, country or region and performance metrics so we can understand reach and improve loading. It is not used here for cross-site advertising profiles.

Game data

Progress, daily challenge completion and sound preferences stay in your browser. The GPT-6 build's daily AI challenge is generated and validated before release by GLM-5.3 Flash; the browser does not send a model key or make a per-frame model request.

The site may send anonymous first-party product events such as a page view, game start, AI challenge load, completion, restart, share click or Paddle checkout lifecycle event. These events help us test whether the model-authored challenge creates useful play and replay. They do not contain a name, email, payment details, chat content or precise location. If the endpoint is unavailable, the event is not sent.

Optional support and advertising

The site may offer an optional support link through Paddle after a live checkout link is configured. The site does not collect or store card details; Paddle handles checkout and payment processing under its own privacy policy and terms.

Google AdSense may be enabled after account and site review. If enabled, Google may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising and measurement. The advertising provider's policies apply, and the configuration can be removed from the site.

Third-party links

Pages may link to GitHub, social networks, payment providers or advertising services after those services are configured. Their own privacy policies apply when you open them.

For questions or a deletion request, contact the maintainer through the public repository.
